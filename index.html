About me

Understanding people.
Designing better robot interactions.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Kyoto University’s Human–Robot Interaction Laboratory. My research explores how robots communicate with people: how they can anticipate feelings, choose socially appropriate language, and support responsible interaction through avatar robots.

Research interests

Human–Robot Interaction, Social Robotics, Affective Computing, Interactive AI Systems, LLM-based Interaction, and Multi-Agent Systems.

Selected research

Three doctoral projects
01 · Robot communication

Robot Knows How to Use Irony Effectively

ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction · 2026

How can a robot use irony in a way that fits the interaction? This project investigates irony as a communication strategy for social robots, connecting the choice of words with the social context in which they are delivered.

中文介绍

机器人如何在合适的情境中使用反讽？这个项目关注机器人语言表达与互动情境的关系，探索反讽作为社交沟通策略的使用方式。

02 · Affective interaction

Meet the Motivational Robot That Predicts Your Future Feelings

ACM Transactions on Human-Robot Interaction · 2025

What might someone feel after a robot tries to motivate them? This project studies future feelings as a basis for motivational interaction, exploring how anticipating an emotional response can inform a robot’s encouragement.

中文介绍

机器人在鼓励一个人之前，能否先考虑对方接下来会有什么感受？这个项目将未来情感预测引入激励式交互，探索如何让机器人的鼓励更关注人的情绪反应。

Read paper ↗
03 · Responsible teleoperation

Don’t Say That! Proactive Support for Appropriate Power Use with Avatar Robot

ACM/IEEE HRI · 2026

An avatar robot can give its remote operator influence over another person. This project explores proactive support for using that influence appropriately. The demonstration contrasts inappropriate criticism during exercise with a supported interaction.

中文介绍

分身机器人让远程操作者能够影响另一端的人。这个项目探索如何通过主动支持，帮助操作者恰当地使用这种影响力。演示视频以运动指导为情境，呈现不恰当批评带来的互动问题。
